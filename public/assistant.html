Ask a question

Supported: today's vat list, one cow's recorded hold, and a calving you want recorded.

Do not enter personal information. The external model, when configured, receives only this text and never receives farm records.

How the answer is produced

  1. The assistant classifies the text into one allowed intent. It never supplies a cow, a date, a medicine or a number.
  2. The server resolves the cow tag and date against the database, and asks when either is missing or ambiguous.
  3. Answers about a hold are read from the stored event snapshot, including the label revision and milking schedule that produced it.
  4. A stated event becomes a draft only. Confirming it saves through the normal treatment route, with the same role checks, validation and audit trail.
  5. Unknown clear dates stay on hold and are shown as needing a person.
