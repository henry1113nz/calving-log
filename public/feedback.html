Suggested test tasks

Use demonstration data and ask the participant to think aloud.

  1. Find every cow whose milk must stay out today.
  2. Record a treatment and explain the calculated dates.
  3. Explain why an unknown date remains on hold.
  4. Schedule a future change from TAD to OAD.
  5. Use SCC evidence to review one dry-off decision.
  6. Ask which cows must stay out of the vat and explain where the result came from.

Safety boundary: this is a student prototype. A participant must not use it to make a real milk-release or treatment decision.
